This document is a licensing route, not a commercial license and not a grant

This includes, without limiting the license text, use in a paid product or
service, SaaS/API offering, OEM or embedded product, revenue-generating
workflow, or internal for-profit business operation.

To request terms, contact the repository owner through the GitHub account
[`@khfy7wpr5p-maker`](https://github.com/khfy7wpr5p-maker) or another
authenticated written channel designated by the licensor. A discussion,
issue, pull request, quote, or unsigned proposal does not grant rights.

## What a ScoreMosaic commercial agreement can cover

A signed agreement may grant defined commercial rights in first-party
ScoreMosaic code and, only if expressly listed, first-party documentation,
data, model artifacts, support, warranty, or trademark permissions. Scope,
term, territory, deployment mode, seats/usage, fees, support, audit, data
protection, warranty, liability, and termination must be agreed separately.

## What it does not automatically cover

A ScoreMosaic commercial agreement cannot relicense third-party components.
The current OMR runtime includes or downloads AGPL/GPL components and model
assets whose separate licensing must be resolved. In particular, Audiveris,
HOMR, Clarity-OMR, PyMuPDF, Ultralytics and related assets may impose source,
network-use, distribution, notice, or separate-commercial-license
obligations. An agreement for ScoreMosaic first-party code does not waive
those obligations.

Commercial production or redistribution remains blocked until the dependency
inventory reports every included component as approved for that exact use.
Trademark, patent, model, dataset, third-party and contributor rights are not
granted unless a signed agreement expressly says so.
